# NestWell Studio Brand Visual Style

## Overall Mood

Organic, warm, handcrafted elegance. The style sits between minimalist Nordic home decor and French countryside warmth: restrained but not cold, refined but not flashy.

Avoid anything metallic, glossy, cyber-futuristic, or reminiscent of high-contrast corporate e-commerce.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary/accent | Terracotta brown | `#9C6B3E` | Logo linework, icons, and button hover states |
| Secondary | Olive green | `#7C8B5F` | Accents, category tags, and icon details |
| Neutral background | Linen off-white | `#F5F0E6`, `#FAF7F0` | Page backgrounds with fabric-like warmth |
| Dark text | Deep coffee brown | `#7A5230`, `#3D2E1F` | Headings and near-black text |
| Supporting gray | Warm taupe | `#9B9691` | Captions, tags, and secondary text |
| Eggshell beige | Eggshell beige | `#D8C7A1` | Card backgrounds, hover states, and section dividers |
| Border | Warm hairline | `#E4DCCB` | Card and module borders |

### Avoid

- Pure white and pure black high-contrast combinations
- Blue or purple hues
- Oversaturated bright colors

## Iconography and Motifs

- **Core motifs:** Bird's nests, thin branch or twig lines, small bird silhouettes, and egg shapes that echo the "nest" in NestWell.
- **Line style:** Organic, hand-drawn curves rather than rigid geometry, with consistent stroke weight and rounded line caps.
- **Icons:** Simple line drawings or minimal silhouettes only. Do not use gradients, drop shadows, or 3D effects.
- **Dividers:** Thin `1px` hairlines and small dots rather than ornate patterns.

## Typography

- **Headlines and logo:** Classic serif type, similar to Georgia or Times New Roman, with `3-6px` letter spacing for a boutique editorial feel.
- **Subheadings and labels:** Serif small caps with wide tracking.
- **Body and UI text:** Clean sans-serif at a light `400` weight. Avoid heavy bold fonts.
- **Taglines and captions:** Small sans-serif with wide letter spacing in muted brown-gray (`#9B9691`).

## Texture

- Backgrounds may include a subtle linen or fabric texture at low opacity to reinforce the handcrafted textile feel. Keep it faint enough to preserve readability.
- Cards and modules use thin `#E4DCCB` hairline borders, a `4-8px` corner radius, and a minimal soft shadow. Avoid heavy drop shadows.

## Layout Principles

- Use generous whitespace and low information density.
- Let icons and illustrations act as visual anchors. For example, use an oversized nest line drawing in a hero section with text wrapping around it.
- Use thin-outline or solid terracotta buttons with a corner radius no greater than `6px` and no gradients.
- Avoid cramped layouts and high-saturation sale or discount energy. The site should feel like a boutique craft studio, not a wholesale storefront.
